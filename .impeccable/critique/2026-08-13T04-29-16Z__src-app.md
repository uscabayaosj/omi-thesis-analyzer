## Overall Impression

A disciplined, deeply product-specific Operate surface at 31/40 with excellent status visibility and honest error handling. Its weak seam is uneven protection of destructive actions and data durability: the most expensive artifacts (analyses) are the least durable data in the system.

## What's Working

1. **Error resilience as UX** — stale-while-revalidate cache with honest copy, skip-and-continue batches, `uriTooLong` fallback to download. Failure modes were designed, not left over.
2. **The parity-action toolbar** (page.tsx + DESIGN.md Buttons) — three-tier disabled/ready/active with measured contrast ratios documented in comments.
3. **Rollup time-of-day chunking** with `CHUNK_THRESHOLD = 6` and a sticky Generate bar — the page understands it serves a depleted end-of-day brain.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Analysis state is localStorage-only.** Fresh browser on prod shows "0/13 analyzed" — every analysis, done-toggle, version, and rollup is per-device. A phone+desktop single user gets two divergent truths and pays twice in API calls. Fix: server-side persistence (Vercel KV/Blob) keyed by conversation id; localStorage becomes cache.
- **[P1] Unguarded destructive re-runs on two of three lenses.** ADHD re-run and group re-run replace instantly — no confirm, no version — while thesis gets both. Fix: reuse ConfirmRerunDialog for all three; batch runs over already-analyzed items should warn they will replace N analyses.
- **[P2] Day selection is ephemeral.** Reload or PWA relaunch dumps to today's (often empty) view. Fix: `?day=...` in the URL for home and rollup.
- **[P2] Batch failures are anonymous.** "3 of 13 could not be analyzed" never names which, no retry-failed. Fix: collect failed titles, list them, add "Retry failed (3)".
- **[P3] Rebrand/polish sweep**: indigo `.analysis-section` wash (detector-confirmed), rollup sticky bar `bg-[#0f172a]` band inside a `#1e293b` card, all-empty ADHD result should collapse to one card, double bottom padding on analysis cards.

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (one-handed mobile)**: calendar day cells `min-h-[40px]` — below the app's own 44px rule on its densest tap grid; "Group Thesis (1)" looks live but eats the tap silently; select-mode titles truncate to ~3 words at 375px.

**Riley (stress-tester)**: reload during a 13-item batch loses progress silently, no resume; regenerating day N−1 after day N chained off it is documented as a hazard rather than detected.

**The researcher at 9pm (project persona)**: the Daily Rollup — the flagship end-of-day ritual — is the app's longest, most fragile wait: serial, uncancelable analysis, then six+ sections of prose with "tomorrow's plan" not surfaced first.

## Minor Observations

- Rollup day buttons: `<li>` inside a `role="list"` div — invalid parentage; day buttons showed empty accessible names in the a11y tree.
- LensBadges is aria-hidden and the row label collapses both lenses to "(analyzed)" — SR users can't tell Thesis-done from ADHD-done.
- Home `analyzedIds` read once at mount; client-nav return can show a stale badge.
- "Both" lens = two buttons, two waits; no run-both affordance.
- Past group analyses have no index; only way back is re-selecting the identical id set.
- Amber "Run Custom Analysis" is a solid amber action fill — the only breach of the One Ink Rule (amber is spec'd as marginalia, not action).

## Questions to Consider

1. If the phone dies the night before a supervision meeting, how much of the dissertation's evidence layer dies with its localStorage — why is the most expensive artifact the least durable data?
2. The rollup dialog admits the chain goes stale rather than fixing it — ethos or debt? What would "regenerate this day and everything downstream" cost?
3. Is custom analysis an aside or an action? The amber fill says both.
